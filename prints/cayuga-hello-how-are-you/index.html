Hello, How Are You?

I'm learning Cayuga right now. The first thing they teach you is how to greet someone — and when you sit with that word in Gayogohó:nǫ', you realize it's not a formality. It carries the whole philosophy of the greeting: to ask how someone is, to acknowledge their presence, to open a door. This is where language begins. In the space between two people.
